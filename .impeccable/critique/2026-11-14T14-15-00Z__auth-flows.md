## 2. Issues & Prioritization

### [P0] Critical (None)
- No blockers or crash-inducing patterns detected.

### [P1] Major / Consistency
1. **Component Inconsistency in `forgot-password/page.tsx`**:
   - The email input is implemented with a raw `<input>` instead of using the project's standard `FormField` component with proper `aria-describedby` and unified styling.
2. **Hardcoded Color Tokens**:
   - `reset-password/page.tsx` uses `bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-500` instead of design tokens (`bg-success/10 text-success`).
   - `PasswordStrength.tsx` uses raw Tailwind colors (`bg-emerald-500`, `bg-amber-500`) instead of semantic tokens (`bg-success`, `bg-warning`).
3. **Touch Targets on Secondary Navigation Links**:
   - Inline navigation links ("نسيت كلمة المرور؟", "سجّل الآن", "سجّل دخولك") have small physical tap areas below the 44px recommended threshold.

### [P2] Minor / Polish
1. **Motion Reduction on Transitions**:
   - `animate-in fade-in-50` in `SuccessState`, `FormError`, and `PasswordStrength` lack `motion-reduce:animate-none`.
2. **BrandSide Terminal Container Contrast**:
   - Ensure the terminal styling in `BrandSide.tsx` cleanly respects high-contrast modes and RTL directionality.
