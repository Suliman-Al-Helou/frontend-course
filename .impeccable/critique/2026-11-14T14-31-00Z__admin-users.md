## 2. Issues & Prioritization

### [P0] Critical (None)
- No runtime blockers or race conditions detected; TanStack Query v5 is cleanly utilized.

### [P1] Major / Consistency
1. **Hardcoded Color Classes**:
   - `StudentsManagementTab.tsx` and `StudentDetailsModal.tsx` use `emerald-500` and `blue-500` instead of semantic design tokens (`text-success`, `bg-success/10`, `border-success/25`, `bg-primary/10`).
2. **Missing Arabic Pluralization**:
   - Total students count (`إجمالي الطلاب: {students.length}`) and course badges (`{paidActiveCount} كورس`, `{freeActiveCount} كورس`) display raw numbers without Arabic pluralization rules.
3. **BiDi Precision on Email Displays**:
   - Emails in student table and modals need explicit `dir="ltr"` to prevent BiDi punctuation flips.

### [P2] Minor / Polish
1. **Touch Target Sizing**:
   - Action buttons in table (ملف الطالب, حذف) have `h-8` (`32px` height) and should be expanded to `h-9 min-h-[36px]` with accessible click target areas.
2. **Motion Reduction on Dialogs**:
   - Verify `motion-reduce:animate-none` across all modals and search input transitions.
